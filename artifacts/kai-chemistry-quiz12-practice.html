Reference - Key Rules

VSEPR Rules

  • 1 domain = 1 bonded atom (regardless of single/double/triple bond)
  • Count lone pairs on the central atom only
  • Total domains = bonding domains + lone pairs
  • More lone pairs → smaller bond angle (LP repels more than BP)
  • Shape name is based on atom positions only, not total domains

IMF Rules

  • ALL molecules: London Dispersion Forces (LDF)
  • Polar molecules: add Dipole-Dipole (DD)
  • N-H, O-H, or F-H bond: add Hydrogen Bonding (HB)
  • Stronger IMF → higher boiling point, slower evaporation
  • Larger molecule → stronger LDF (more surface area)
