ERRORS — DETAILED ANALYSIS
6.3.3 Conversion Factor Terminology vocabulary stoichiometry NP

Explain which conversion factors you used in the 3.50 L O2 → g MgO problem and why.

Kai’s Answer
“Used Molality of liquid to go from L to mol”
Correct Answer
Molar Volume (22.4 L/mol at STP) converts L ↔ mol for a gas. Molality is a concentration unit (mol/kg solvent) — completely different concept.
Root Cause
Kai knows the calculation (3.50 ÷ 22.4 is correct) but misnamed the conversion factor. Molality and molar volume sound similar but are completely different: Molality = mol solute per kg solvent (solution concentration). Molar Volume = liters per mole of gas at STP (22.4 L/mol). One term to memorize: the factor that connects L ↔ mol for a gas is always Molar Volume.
