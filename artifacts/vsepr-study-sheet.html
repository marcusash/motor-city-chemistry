Core VSEPR Rules

  1. Electron groups repel and spread as far apart as possible.
  2. Single, double, and triple bonds each count as one electron region.
  3. Lone pairs only affect geometry when they sit on the central atom.
  4. Lone pairs repel more strongly than bonding pairs.
  5. More lone pairs → more compression → smaller bond angles.
  6. Electron geometry counts bonds and lone pairs.
  7. Molecular geometry is the visible arrangement of atoms only.

How to Count “# Centers”

# Centers = Bonding Directions + Lone Pairs on Central Atom
  • Each bond counts as 1, whether single, double, or triple.
  • Each lone pair on the central atom counts as 1.
  • Lone pairs on outer atoms (e.g., the O’s in CO2) do not count.
  • 2 centers → linear (180°).
  • 3 centers → trig. planar (120°), or bent if 1 LP.
  • 4 centers → tetrahedral (109.5°), pyr. tet. (1 LP), or tet. bent (2 LP).
