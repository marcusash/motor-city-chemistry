Worked Example — The Fraction Move

Balance combustion equations by saving O2 for last. If you need an odd number of O atoms, write a fractional O2 coefficient, then multiply every coefficient by the denominator to clear the fraction.

H₂ + O₂ → H₂O
Step 1. Balance H → 1 H2O gives 2 H ✓
Step 2. Balance O → right side has 1 O, but O2 supplies 2 → write ½ O2: H₂ + ½ O₂ → H₂O
Step 3. Multiply every coefficient × 2 to remove the fraction: 2H₂ + O₂ → 2H₂O
Check: H = 4 = 4 ✓ O = 2 = 2 ✓
