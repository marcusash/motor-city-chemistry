Chemistry — Fraction Balancing Drill

Unit 6.1 · Combustion equations with odd O counts

~20 minutes. Show all work. Balance one element at a time and save O2 for last.

Worked Example — The Fraction Move

When balancing combustion, save O2 for last. If you need an odd number of O atoms, write a fractional O2 coefficient (like ½ or 5/2), then multiply every coefficient by the denominator to clear the fraction.

H2 + O2 → H2O

Step 1. Balance H → 1 H2O gives 2 H ✓

Step 2. Balance O → right side has 1 O, but O2 supplies 2 → write ½ O2:

H2 + ½ O2 → H2O

Step 3. Multiply every coefficient × 2 to clear the fraction:

2H2 + O2 → 2H2O

Check: H = 4 = 4 ✓ O = 2 = 2 ✓
