Didn't criss-cross the charges. Ba is +2 and PO₄ is -3. Diagnosis: What Kai Needs to Study

  1. Ionic vs Covalent naming (4.2.6, FeCl₂)
    This is Kai's #1 gap right now. He is applying covalent prefixes (mono-, di-, tri-) to ionic compounds. The rule is simple: if the first element is a metal, it's ionic, and you NEVER use prefixes. Prefixes are only for compounds where BOTH elements are nonmetals (like CO₂ = carbon dioxide). This caused errors on Q7 Rows 1, 2, and 3.
  2. Calculating transition metal charge from formula (FeCl₂)
    Kai wrote Iron(I) but it should be Iron(II). The method: figure out the total negative charge from the anions (2 Cl⁻ = -2), then the metal must be the opposite (+2). The Roman numeral matches the metal's charge, not the number of anions.
  3. Criss-cross method for unequal charges (AlCl₃, Ba₃(PO₄)₂)
    When the charges aren't equal, the number from one charge becomes the subscript of the other ion. Kai needs to practice this for cases where the charges don't cancel 1:1. Always check by multiplying charge x subscript and confirming the total is zero.
