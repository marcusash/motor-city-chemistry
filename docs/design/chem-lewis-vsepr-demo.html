A. Lewis Dot Structures

Lines = bonding pairs (shared electrons). Dot pairs ( •• ) = lone (nonbonding) pairs. Each line = 2 electrons. Each dot pair = 2 electrons.
Water — H₂O
8 val e⁻ · 2 bond pairs · 2 lone pairs
Ammonia — NH₃
8 val e⁻ · 3 bond pairs · 1 lone pair
Methane — CH₄
8 val e⁻ · 4 bond pairs · 0 lone pairs
Carbon Dioxide — CO₂
16 val e⁻ · 2 double bonds · 2 LP per O
Hydrogen Chloride — HCl
8 val e⁻ · 1 bond pair · 3 LP on Cl
Nitrogen Gas — N₂
10 val e⁻ · 1 triple bond · 1 LP per N
Oxygen Gas — O₂
12 val e⁻ · 1 double bond · 2 LP per O
Fluorine Gas — F₂
14 val e⁻ · 1 bond pair · 3 LP per F

B. VSEPR Molecular Geometry

Electron geometry (counts lone pairs) vs. molecular geometry (shape of atoms only). Lone pairs on the central atom compress bond angles below the ideal.

D. Polarity & Dipole Moments

Polar bonds form when electronegativity differs. Net dipole = vector sum of all bond dipoles. Symmetric molecules cancel to zero = nonpolar molecule. Symbol: δ+ (partial positive), δ− (partial negative). Arrow points from δ+ toward δ−.
Hydrogen Fluoride (HF)
POLAR — asymmetric, net dipole ≠ 0
Water (H₂O)
POLAR — bent shape, dipoles don't cancel
Carbon Dioxide (CO₂)
NONPOLAR — linear, dipoles cancel
Technology Notes (FA dev prototype):
SmilesDrawer v2.3.0 (Section C): Open source, MIT license, CDN via unpkg.com. Input = SMILES strings. Renders to HTML canvas. Zero server dependency. Best for organic/covalent structural formulas with accurate bond geometry and ring systems. Not suitable for Lewis dot structures or polarity diagrams.
Inline SVG (Sections A, B, D): No dedicated JavaScript library exists for Lewis electron dot diagrams or VSEPR geometry. Hand-crafted SVG is the standard approach. Finite molecule set for high school chem (~20-30 common molecules). Once built, SVG components are reusable across all quiz files. Recommended next step: extract SVGs into a shared component library.
Libraries evaluated and not recommended for this use case: RDKit.js (35MB WASM, overkill), Kekule.js (full cheminformatics toolkit, no Lewis dots), OpenChemLib JS (SMILES-to-SVG, no Lewis dots), 3Dmol.js (interactive 3D, doesn't print).
